HOW TO PLAY

Goal: collect every crystal in a world, defeat its boss, return to the village, travel on.
Desktop: WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, mouse look, click attack, right-click block, H potion, E interact, Q/R/T abilities, Esc pause.
Mobile: left stick moves, drag the right side to look. Combat buttons appear only when enemies are near.
